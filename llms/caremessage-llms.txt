# CareMessage

> CareMessage is a healthcare technology non-profit operating one of the largest patient engagement platforms for low-income and underserved populations in the United States. Since 2013 it has reached 20M+ patients and is used by ~1 in 7 federally qualified health centers and 200+ free clinics. CareMessage does not currently publish a public developer API, SDK, or API reference; the platform is accessed through its web application and a login-gated help center.

## Platform

- [Our Platform](https://www.caremessage.org/our-platform/): Patient engagement platform — 1:1 direct messaging, appointment lifecycle messaging, population outreach, health coaching, closed-loop referrals, and SDOH workflows.
- [Wellness Copilot](https://www.caremessage.org/our-platform/wellness-copilot/): AI-backed diabetes-management automation.
- [Platform Login](https://app.caremessage.org/): Web application sign-in for customer organizations.
